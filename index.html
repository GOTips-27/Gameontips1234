🏇 Bangalore Race Previews

27 November 2026 · Seven-race meeting

PROVISIONAL AI ANALYSIS

Race 1 – Kunchikal Falls Plate Div. II

2:00 PM · 1100m · Class IV

Contenders to assess: Windcleaver (4), Zayana (2), Black Rainforest (5), Anzac Parade (6).

Windcleaver's 600m workout in 43 seconds was described as stretched. Zayana recorded a 1200m workout in 1:23 and a good jump. Anzac Parade also showed encouraging older workouts.

Fitness caution: disclosed health and injury concerns were reported for some contenders. Verify current fitness before assessment.

Race 2 – The Becket Plate

1200m · Three-year-old maiden

Contenders to assess: Aristocratic Queen, Darringer, Knotty Tale, Shining Path.

Recent supplied workouts included encouraging efforts from these runners.

Maiden races can be unpredictable. Confirm the complete field, jockeys and final racecard.

Race 3 – Hyderabad Silver Vase

1600m · Class I

Contenders to assess: Born To Rise and Victor Hugo.

Born To Rise recorded a notable 1000m workout in 1:07. Victor Hugo featured in supplied paired-work reports.

Full racecard, recent form and runner confirmation are required.

Race 4 – V. Venugopal Naidu Memorial Trophy

1400m · Class III

Contenders to assess: Forseti, Star Of India, Deep Respect, Jade Mountain.

Supplied workout reports described useful preparation from these runners.

Deep Respect has a disclosed injury. Check current fitness and final declarations.

Race 5 – Bangalore 1000 Guineas

1600m · Grade II · Fillies

Contenders to assess: Starzella, Fine Silver, Moon Oracle, Moonveil.

Supplied preparation reports included useful longer-distance work from these fillies.

Check the full field, recent form and final declarations before ranking.

Race 6 – Kunchikal Falls Plate Div. I

4:30 PM · 1100m · Class IV

Main contender to assess: Agrador (5).

Alternatives: Pandurr (7), Agera (2), Lauterbrunnen (4).

Supplied reports included fast workouts and mock-race information for these horses.

Medical cautions were disclosed for several contenders. Verify their current status.

Race 7 – Snap Plate

5:00 PM · 1100m · Class III

Main contender to assess: Seize The Grey (12).

Alternatives: Siege Storm (6), Auspicious King (7), Deep Respect (9).

Supplied workout reports included longer-distance preparation and paired exercises.

Check health disclosures, final declarations and fitness information.
