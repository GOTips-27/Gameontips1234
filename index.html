🏇 Bangalore Race Previews

27 November 2026 · Seven-race meeting

PROVISIONAL AI ANALYSIS

Race 1 – Kunchikal Falls Plate Div. II

2:00 PM · 1100m · Class IV · 4yo and older

Contenders to assess: Windcleaver (4), Zayana (2), Black Rainforest (5), Anzac Parade (6).

Windcleaver's 600m workout in 43 seconds was described as stretched. Zayana recorded a 1200m workout in 1:23 and a good jump. Anzac Parade also showed encouraging older workouts, including a 600m effort in 40 seconds.

Fitness caution: Zayana has disclosed suspensory and myopathy issues. Windcleaver has a recent injury disclosure and respiratory history. Anzac Parade also has a sore-back disclosure. These concerns make confidence limited.

Race 2 – The Becket Plate

1200m · Three-year-old maiden

Contenders to assess: Aristocratic Queen, Darringer, Knotty Tale, Shining Path.

Aristocratic Queen's recent 1000m and 1200m workouts were encouraging. Darringer completed a 1200m workout in 1:24. Knotty Tale also showed good paired-work reports. Shining Path has recorded useful 1000m and 600m efforts.

Maiden races can be unpredictable. Confirm the complete field, jockeys and final racecard before publishing a ranking.

Race 3 – Hyderabad Silver Vase

1600m · Class I · Rating 80 and above

Contenders to assess: Born To Rise and Victor Hugo.

Born To Rise recorded a notable 1000m workout in 1:07 and a 600m section in 41 seconds. Victor Hugo finished ahead of several horses in the supplied paired-work reports.

Exotic Black, Kiss N Tell and Hyperdrive were among the horses involved in those comparisons.

Provisional assessment only. Full racecard, recent form and runner confirmation are required before a final ranking.

Race 4 – V. Venugopal Naidu Memorial Trophy

1400m · Class III · Rating 40–65

Contenders to assess: Forseti, Star Of India, Deep Respect, Jade Mountain.

Forseti recorded encouraging 1400m workouts. Star Of India showed useful preparation over 1200m. Deep Respect worked with Glorious Strides, with Deep Respect reported better in their latest paired exercise. Jade Mountain also moved well in recent work.

Deep Respect has a disclosed injury. Check the complete racecard and current fitness before finalising the analysis.

Race 5 – Bangalore 1000 Guineas

1600m · Grade II · Fillies

Contenders to assess: Starzella, Fine Silver, Moon Oracle, Moonveil.

Starzella's preparation included a 1400m workout in 1:38 and a 1000m effort in 1:07. Fine Silver recorded useful 1200m workouts. Moon Oracle completed repeated longer-distance work, while Moonveil also had a 1600m workout in its preparation.

This is a stakes race. Full field, recent form and final declarations must be checked before making a final assessment.

Race 6 – Kunchikal Falls Plate Div. I

4:30 PM · 1100m · Class IV · Rating 20–45

Main contender to assess: Agrador (5).

Alternatives: Pandurr (7), Agera (2), Lauterbrunnen (4).

Agrador recorded repeated fast 1000m workouts, including 1:06 and 1:07 efforts. Pandurr impressed in a mock race and recorded a 600m workout in 39 seconds. Agera also recorded a quick 600m workout. Lauterbrunnen was described as impressive in a mock race.

Medical cautions: Agrador has an airway-related disclosure. Pandurr has tendon history and recent health disclosures. Agera has a previous knee surgery. Lauterbrunnen has a past bleeding disclosure.

Race 7 – Snap Plate

5:00 PM · 1100m · Class III · Rating 40–65

Main contender to assess: Seize The Grey (12).

Alternatives: Siege Storm (6), Auspicious King (7), Deep Respect (9).

Seize The Grey completed repeated longer-distance workouts and was reported to have stretched well. Siege Storm finished five lengths ahead of Refined Aggression in paired work. Auspicious King recorded a 600m workout in 39 seconds. Deep Respect was reported better than Glorious Strides in their latest paired work.

Medical cautions: Siege Storm has previous health disclosures. Deep Respect has an injury disclosure. Check all final declarations and fitness information.
